Chouwazi is an offline random selection tool in the Utilities category for people using the same device. All selection processing takes place on the device. No account, internet connection or review credentials are needed. The app has no advertising or in-app purchases.

How to test on a physical iPhone or iPad with multi-touch input:
1. Place 2 to 10 fingers in the selection area, away from the control buttons, and keep holding.
2. After a 0.8-second preparation period and a 3-second countdown, one participating finger is selected at random. Each participating finger has an equal chance, and the result stays on screen.
3. Before a result is selected, adding or lifting a participating finger restarts both preparation and the countdown. Moving a finger does not restart the timer.
4. Lift every finger from the screen, including any fingers beyond the 10-finger limit or added after the result appeared, then tap “Another round” to start again.
5. With all fingers lifted, the haptics control changes vibration feedback. The Privacy button opens the bundled policy and works offline.

A single touch waits for another participant, so a single mouse pointer alone does not demonstrate the selection flow. The device's supported simultaneous touch count and system gesture settings may limit the number of participants. Haptic feedback depends on device support and system settings.

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese and follows the device language. It supports both iPhone and iPad.
