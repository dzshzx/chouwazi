Chouwazi is a local multi-touch random selector. It does not require an account, a server or an internet connection. No review credentials are needed.

How to test on an iPhone or iPad:
1. Place at least two fingers on the screen and keep holding.
2. After preparation and the countdown, one participating finger is selected.
3. Adding or lifting a participating finger restarts the countdown. Moving a finger does not.
4. Lift every finger, then tap “Another round” to start again.
5. The haptics control changes vibration feedback. The Privacy button opens the bundled policy and works offline.

A single touch waits for another participant, so a single mouse pointer alone does not demonstrate the selection flow. The app has no wagers, payments, prizes, redemption, advertising or in-app purchases.

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese and follows the device language. It supports both iPhone and iPad.
